# Horizon v2 reference designs

Reference implementations for the A20 build. Port their drawing code, timings and layout into apps/web. They are not app code and are not imported anywhere.

- `Main.dc.html`: live hero. Line draw-in, sphere assembly, light drift, stats strip and latest-proof line.
- `Explainer.dc.html`: "Watch a payment cross the line". The full 10-second timeline is in `draw(t)`.
- `Groups.dc.html`: (superseded by GroupsOrbit.dc.html, kept for its sizing and density code) groups as spheres. Sizing (`radius`), tier density (`step`, shadow floor) and staggered assembly.
- `GroupsOrbit.dc.html`: **the chosen Groups page, replacing `Groups.dc.html`.** Every group sits on a tilted (-10°) dotted orbit, depth-scaled, with back bodies small and faded. Clicking a body or using the arrows rotates the orbit with easing until it reaches the front, where it grows to its true size (by proven calls, density by tier) and assembles. The info panel (rank, proven, tier, fee, refused, progress to next tier) sits bottom-left, filters (All / Groups / Solo builders) top-right, and the counter, arrows and Open group link bottom-right. It only moves when the user acts, never on a loop.
- `Share.dc.html`: Your share. Personal sphere seeded from the account name (`seed`, `drawMark`) and the value count-up. The "Your mark, drawn from your account name" caption is deleted here and everywhere in the app.
- `Wallet.dc.html`: (dropdown superseded by the account menu in BalancePage.dc.html; its pieces now live on the Balance page) the "Your wallet" panel redesign, approved. Desktop popover anchored under the account button, phone bottom sheet. Personal sphere header on the -10° line, "Your share is worth" headline, plain USDC and WARD rows with a quiet NEAR-for-fees line, a full-width primary, the last transaction, and a quiet footer (Your share, Switch wallet, Disconnect).
- `Buttons.dc.html`: primary button options. **Option I, "Sheen", is chosen** for every primary button site-wide: a dark fill, a 1px edge running white to violet (`#F5F3F7` to `#7B6CFF` to `#4B3BD9`), and on hover a single light beam passing across at -10°. The `.i-btn` CSS there is the spec, and `.sheen` in Wallet.dc.html is the same style. Options G and H are rejected, kept only for reference.
- `Developers.dc.html`: **the approved Developers page.** A "What are you building?" picker (an agent that calls AI models / an agent that pays vendors / my own app) drives one code panel with language tabs, a Copy button and "the one line" highlighted, plus "What you get back" and "Also for this" links. Its snippets are illustrative, so use the real ones from Connect.tsx. The page leaves the main nav (see the UI list).
- `Cards.dc.html`: card treatments. **Option 6, "The line is the layout", is chosen**: no box at all. The -10° line runs across the section, the "good" number sits above it top-left and the red number below it bottom-right, each with a short plain label, with big numbers (about 104px) and lots of empty space. Use it for every paired stat block (the above/below tally, the agent alone vs with Wardline comparison, proof stats). Site-wide rule: no boxed rounded "AI slop" cards. Content blocks use open layouts (hairline dividers, generous spacing, type-led hierarchy, as in option 4) instead of filled panels. Options 4 and 5 stay for reference.
- `BalancePage.dc.html`: **the chosen header control, account menu and Balance page (clickable).** Header: the "Joined" control, one connected pill with "Balance 20.00 USDC" on the left (opens the Balance page, violet underline + lighter fill when you are on it) and the mini sphere + account name + chevron on the right (opens the account menu). `.seg` CSS is the spec. Account menu: Connected with Meteor, Open your balance, Copy account name, Connect a different wallet, Disconnect. Balance page: merges Your share and the wallet panel pieces (share value, USDC and WARD rows, quiet NEAR-for-fees line, Sheen primary, transactions). Lives at `#/wallet`; `#/me` (Your share) redirects there.
- `BalanceButtons.dc.html`: Balance button options. **Option 3, "Joined", is chosen** (same as BalancePage.dc.html). Options 1 (Ticker) and 2 (Sheen) are reference only.
- `BalanceDollars.dc.html`: **the chosen Balance page top, in dollars.** Replaces the "160.00 USDC total" block in BalancePage.dc.html. Big "$160.00" (small muted $ and cents), "Your share has earned +$0.01 since you joined", a two-part bar (white "Ready to deposit $140.00", violet "Earning in the Mutual $20.00") that grows in on load, and rows that lead with dollars with the token amount small under them (USDC "Digital dollars. 1 USDC is always $1", WARD "Each one is worth $1.0004 today"). NEAR stays in NEAR and is not counted in the balance.
- `stewdio-preview.html`: frame previewer for the two Stewdio animations (Horizon hero, Payment crosses the line), with the same scene timings.

These are design-canvas files (`.dc.html`). Markup is in `<x-dc>`, logic is in the `class Component` script, and holes like `{{x}}` come from `renderVals()`. The `sphere()` function in each file is the shared halftone renderer, and should match the app's `horizonDots` look.

The numbers shown (12.15 USDC, 46 proven calls, and so on) are a snapshot of NEAR testnet on Nov 10, 2026. The app must read live data instead.
