Wardline is a credit union for the AI economy. Members pool USDC on NEAR, back AI agents, and earn from every payment that proves itself. Anything that can't prove itself stays below the line.

## The line

Every payment an AI agent makes lands on one side of a line.

- **Above the line:** the payment is proven and paid. The provider is paid, and a small fee goes to the pool.
- **Below the line:** the payment is refused, blocked or capped, and the money stays in the budget.

Nothing is paid on trust. A model call is paid only when a signed receipt shows that the model you asked for produced that exact answer.

## Three layers

| Layer | What it does | Read next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Mutual | Anyone deposits USDC and holds WARD, a share of the pool. WARD earns a share of every proven payment and covers a wrong approval after Wardline's own stake. | [Deposit and WARD](#/docs/deposit-and-ward) |
| Groups | A team, hacker house or DAO funds a shared AI budget. Members and their agents spend from it, inside allowances. A clean record lowers the fee. | [Groups and budgets](#/docs/groups-and-budgets) |
| The proof engine | Checks the model that ran, the party that gets paid, and the limit that holds, before money moves. | [How a payment is proven](#/docs/how-it-works) |

## Who it is for

- **People who build agents.** Change one line in your OpenAI client and every call is paid only on proof. Start with the [Quickstart](#/docs/quickstart).
- **People who back the pool.** Deposit USDC, hold WARD and vote on the risks the pool takes. Start with [Deposit and WARD](#/docs/deposit-and-ward).
- **Teams that spend on AI.** Give each member an allowance and a budget that a leaked key can't exceed. Start with [The spending cap](#/docs/spending-cap).

:::note
Wardline runs on NEAR testnet. Nothing here uses real money. [Limits and risks](#/docs/limits-and-risks) says plainly what is and isn't covered.
:::
