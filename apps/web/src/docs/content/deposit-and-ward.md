Anyone with a NEAR wallet can join the Mutual. You deposit USDC and receive WARD, a token that holds your share of the pool. No group is needed.

## What you get

WARD is a fungible token on NEAR (NEP-141), named "Wardline Mutual share", with six decimals. It does three things:

- **Earns.** WARD's price rises with the fees the pool collects. See [Fees and yield](#/docs/fees-and-yield).
- **Covers.** If Wardline approves a payment it shouldn't have, WARD holders cover the loss after Wardline's own stake. See [Loss coverage](#/docs/loss-coverage).
- **Votes.** One WARD is one vote on fees, coverage and the sell wait. See [Governance](#/docs/governance).

## The price

WARD's price is the USDC that belongs to WARD holders divided by the WARD in circulation. It started at 1 USDC. It rises as fees come in and falls only when a payout goes past Wardline's stake.

When you deposit, you receive WARD at today's price. At a price of 1.0114 USDC, 25.00 USDC buys 24.72 WARD.

## Become a member

1. Open [Your balance](#/wallet) and choose **Become a member**.
2. Connect a NEAR wallet.
3. Choose an amount. The smallest deposit is 1.00 USDC. The app shows how much WARD you will receive and how much of Wardline's own stake sits in front of yours.
4. Approve the transaction in your wallet.

The deposit is a real transaction from your wallet. You sign it yourself, and Wardline never holds your keys.

## Before you deposit

You need two things in the wallet:

- **USDC.** Circle's USDC on NEAR. On testnet, Circle's faucet gives you some.
- **A little NEAR for network fees.** About 0.05 NEAR covers a deposit.

:::important
WARD is yield-bearing and loss-sharing. It is not stable and nothing about it is guaranteed. The Mutual is protocol-backed coverage, not insurance. Read [Limits and risks](#/docs/limits-and-risks) before you deposit.
:::

## After you deposit

Your balance page shows what you hold in dollars: USDC ready to deposit and WARD earning in the Mutual. Moving WARD between wallets works like any NEAR token, and the pool reports each move, so wallets and explorers show it.
