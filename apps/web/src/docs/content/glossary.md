title: Glossary
description: The words the docs and the app use, in one place.
---

Terms are in alphabetical order.

## A to F

**Above the line.** Proven and paid.

**Below the line.** Refused, blocked or capped, with the money kept in the budget or refunded.

**Allowance.** The most one member can spend from a budget in a period: a day, a week or a month.

**Budget.** A group's deposit of USDC that its members spend from. See [Groups and budgets](#/docs/groups-and-budgets).

**Cover fee.** The share of a proven payment that goes to the pool. It is set by the group's tier. See [Fees and yield](#/docs/fees-and-yield).

**Evidence tier.** How much has been verified about a payee, from `T0_NONE` to `T4_EXTERNAL_KYB`. It never claims legal ownership. See [Payee checks](#/docs/payee-checks).

**First-loss stake.** Wardline's own USDC in the pool. A payout draws on it before any WARD holder's capital.

## G to P

**Group.** A team, hacker house, DAO or solo builder that spends from a shared budget.

**Meter key.** The key an agent holds, one per budget member, that starts with `wk_`. It calls the gateway.

**Mutual.** The pool of USDC that backs payments and pays back a wrong approval.

**NEP-141.** NEAR's fungible token standard. WARD follows it.

**Proven.** A model call whose response was signed by a signer attested for the model that was asked for.

## Q to Z

**Quorum.** The share of WARD that must vote for a vote to count: 20% of the supply at the time the proposal opened.

**Reservation.** The most a call can cost, held back while the call runs and released in full if it is not proven.

**Sell wait.** The wait between asking to redeem WARD and receiving USDC. Seven days by default. See [Redeem and the sell wait](#/docs/redeem-and-sell-wait).

**Snapshot weight.** A holder's voting weight, taken from their WARD balance when a proposal opens, so moving WARD afterwards can't vote twice.

**TEE.** A trusted execution environment: hardware that signs what ran inside it.

**Tier.** A group's record: Bronze, Silver or Gold. It sets the fee and the coverage cap. See [Tiers and reputation](#/docs/tiers-and-reputation).

**USDC.** Circle's dollar token on NEAR, with six decimals. All money in Wardline is USDC.

**WARD.** The token that holds your share of the Mutual. See [Deposit and WARD](#/docs/deposit-and-ward).
